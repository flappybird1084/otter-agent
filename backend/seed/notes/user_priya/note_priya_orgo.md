# Orgo mechanisms cheat sheet

The arrows always go from filled to empty. If you remember nothing else, remember that.

- **SN1 vs SN2**: SN2 is concerted, backside attack, inverts stereo. SN1 is two-step, racemizes.
- **E1 vs E2**: E2 needs antiperiplanar H. E1 goes through a carbocation.
- **Markovnikov**: H goes to the carbon with more Hs already (i.e., carbocation forms on the more-substituted side).
- **Anti-Markovnikov**: peroxides → radical mechanism → opposite regio.
