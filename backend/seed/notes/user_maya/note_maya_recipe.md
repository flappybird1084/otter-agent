# Mom's dal recipe

For when I'm homesick.

- 1 cup masoor dal, rinsed
- 3 cups water, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp salt — simmer 25 min until soft
- Tadka: ghee, cumin seeds, hing, 2 dried chillies, garlic — when fragrant pour over the dal
- Finish: lemon juice, cilantro

She always says "by feel" for spices. Approximately: more than I think.
