# CS109 — probability review

Stuff to internalize before the next pset:

- **Bayes' rule** in odds form is easier than fractions
- **Linearity of expectation** works even when events are dependent
- **Markov / Chebyshev / Chernoff** — order of tightness
- **Indicator variables** for counting questions

I always confuse covariance and correlation. Cov scales with units, corr is normalized. The sign tells you the same thing, the magnitude doesn't.
